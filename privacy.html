Runlow - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Runlow is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Runlow does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no Runlow account, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Everything the app does happens on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Sign-in credentials. When you sign into Claude, you do so through Anthropic's official login page shown inside the app. The resulting session token is stored only in your device's secure Keychain. For Codex or Gemini you may paste an access token from your own command-line tools; that token is also stored only in the Keychain.
  • Usage data. The app requests your usage limits directly from the provider you connected (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google), using your own credentials, and displays them to you. This data is cached on your device so widgets and the Live Activity can show it. It is never sent anywhere else.
  • Settings. Your preferences (colours, chosen meters, notification thresholds, etc.) are stored on your device.

Network connections

Runlow connects only to the services you choose to connect:

  • Anthropic (claude.ai) - to read your Claude usage.
  • OpenAI (chatgpt.com) - to read your Codex usage, if connected.
  • Google (cloudcode-pa.googleapis.com) - to read your Gemini CLI usage, if connected.
  • Public status pages (status.claude.com, status.openai.com, status.cloud.google.com) - to show whether a provider is having an outage. These requests contain no personal information.

Runlow has no servers of its own and sends no data to the developer.

Notifications

If you enable them, alerts (e.g. at 80% / 95% or when a limit resets) are generated and shown locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Children

Runlow does not target children and collects no data from anyone.
