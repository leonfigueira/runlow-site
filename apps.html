One job.
Done properly.

Why it works this way

Free to start, and paid for once, on purpose.

Weekly subscriptions now drive most of the money on the App Store, and a lot of them are charging by the week for something your phone can already almost do. That model needs you to forget you're paying. These do not work that way: most are free to download, and anything extra is a single purchase you keep, so if a new version is better, you already have it. The exceptions are two professional tools built on live government data. GainScout offers a Pro subscription alongside reports you buy one at a time, and Merestone offers a subscription for every country alongside a one-off unlock per country. Both say so plainly.

1

No account, ever

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to, no email address collected. Open the app and it works.

2

There is no server

Every one of these apps does its work on your device. Your files, scripts and takes never travel anywhere, except through your own iCloud.

3

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and nothing sold to anyone. Recast offers an advert, provided by Google, that you can choose to watch in exchange for an extra file. It is never required, Recast never asks to track you, and Recast Pro removes adverts altogether.

The promise

Buy it once. It keeps working.

No trial that quietly becomes a charge. No feature you already paid for moving behind a new tier. If one of these apps stops earning its place on your home screen, delete it, and that is the end of it.
