Why it works this way

A subscription is a good deal for exactly one of us.

Weekly subscriptions now drive most of the money on the App Store, and a lot of them are charging by the week for something your phone can already almost do. That model needs you to forget you're paying. These apps don't: you pay once, and if a new version is better, you already have it.

1

No account, ever

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to, no email address collected. Open the app and it works.

2

There is no server

Every one of these apps does its work on your device. Your files, scripts and takes never travel anywhere — except through your own iCloud, if you turn that on.

3

No tracking, no ads

No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no behavioural data. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected" because that is true.

The promise

Buy it once. It keeps working.

No trial that quietly becomes a charge. No feature you already paid for moving behind a new tier. No app that stops opening because a subscription lapsed. If one of these apps stops earning its place on your home screen, delete it — you won't be billed again either way.
