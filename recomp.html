Pick a file and Recomp shows you two numbers: how big it is now, and how big it will be. Choose a target — 10 MB for Discord, 16 MB for WhatsApp, 25 MB for email, or any size you like — and press one button.

What it shrinks

"Too large to attach" is a solved problem. It just isn't solved honestly very often.

The App Store is full of storage cleaners charging by the week for this. Recomp does the job with Apple's own frameworks, on your device, for one payment.

▾

Video

Hardware-accelerated re-encoding aimed at your exact target size, with an optional "force it smaller" mode for when every megabyte counts.

▾

Photos

JPEG and HEIC compressed to a size you choose, rather than a vague quality slider you have to guess at.

▾

PDFs

Compression that keeps text sharp where it can, so the document is still readable at the other end.

▾

GIFs & audio

GIFs become tiny MP4s that still autoplay everywhere. Audio re-encodes to AAC at the size you need.

▾

Everything else

Documents, spreadsheets, project files — anything else gets zipped. Pick several at once and they become one tidy archive.

▾

No fake numbers

It warns you before it starts if a format has to change, tells you afterwards exactly how much you saved, and says plainly when it couldn't hit your target.

The promise

Pay once. There is no server.

Everything happens on your device. No uploads, no account, no tracking, no subscription. Files you compress never leave your phone — which is the part the free web compressors would rather you didn't think about.
