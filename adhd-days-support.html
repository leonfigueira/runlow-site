Start small

Add one reminder

Tap the centre plus button. Choose Simple for a quick reminder or Detailed when you want timing, progress, notes, photos or a voice note.

Flexible routines

Start from when you woke up

Tap Start day and choose your routine. Flexible routines follow the time you actually start, while clock routines can use fixed times.

A missed window

Expired is not deleted

Optional expiry moves an unanswered reminder out of Today. Open Expired or History to review it and return it when needed.

Projects

Count partial progress

Detailed reminders can track sessions or amounts. Adding progress moves the project forward without marking the whole reminder finished.

Your records

Logbook stays separate from reminders

Use Logbook for something that already happened, such as where you put an item or when you completed a household check. Add notes, a timestamp, and optional location or media. Use Tallies for repeated events and optional quantities.

Notifications

If an alert does not arrive

Check iOS Settings, Notifications, and any active Focus mode. Open ADHD Days periodically so upcoming repeating alerts can refresh.

Widgets

One thing on your Home Screen

Add an ADHD Days widget from the iOS widget gallery. Tapping a reminder opens the app and asks before completing it.

ADHD Days Pro

£4.99 once. No subscription.

Pro is a permanent unlock for the Apple Account that bought it. The separate 21-day gift is free, can be used once, and never becomes an automatic charge. Use Restore purchases on the Pro page after reinstalling or changing devices.

Backup

Moving or preserving your data

Export a full backup to include attachments. A records-only export excludes photos, videos and audio. ADHD Days has no account and no in-app cloud sync.
