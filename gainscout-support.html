GainScout - Support

GainScout helps you take a first look at whether a piece of land in England could be a candidate for Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG). Point the map at a field and one tap reads that spot against 16 official government datasets - designations, flood risk, farmland grade, nature recovery and the registered gain-sites market - then turns it into a plain-English result and, if you want, a shareable report. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Need help or found a bug? Email leonfigueira@gmail.com, or use report a bug or send an idea in the app's Account tab, which fills in your app version for you.

Getting started

  • Move the map over the land you're interested in - the pin marks the exact spot that gets checked. Tap Check this land.
  • Read the result: what's in your favour, what to be aware of, each with its official source and the date it was checked. Save the site to add visit notes, photos and a report.
  • On Pro, Area Scan ranks the best spots across an area, draw your parcel measures a boundary and checks it spot by spot, and you can browse the nearest registered gain sites.

Common questions

Is this the same data as the government sites? Yes - every finding comes from official, public data (planning.data.gov.uk and Natural England open data), live, with its source and date shown. The gain-sites register is a dated snapshot bundled in the app so it works offline. It is guidance for your own research, not an ecological survey, a statutory biodiversity metric calculation, or legal, planning or financial advice - always confirm on the ground and with qualified professionals before relying on it.

What do I pay for? Your first report is free, and you get 5 land checks a week free. After that you can buy reports one at a time or in a pack, or subscribe to Pro for unlimited checks and reports plus the professional tools (Area Scan, draw-your-parcel, the gain-sites browser and side-by-side comparison). We deliberately call these "reports", not "credits" - in BNG, credits means something specific and different.

I accidentally used a report I didn't mean to. Email me and I'll send you a one-time support code that adds it back. Redeem it in Account → "Have a support code?".

Will my sites move to a new phone? Turn on iCloud sync in Account and your sites, reports and photos stay in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. You can also use Back up everything to save a single file you can restore on another device. Pro follows your Apple ID automatically; if you switch phones with reports left over, email me for a support code.

Does it cover Scotland and Wales? Not yet - BNG and these datasets are England-only, so a check outside England is refused rather than guessed.

A check said some sources couldn't be reached. One or more official services didn't respond, so the check is marked incomplete - run it again on a better connection before relying on it.
