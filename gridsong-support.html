Gridsong - Support

Gridsong is a musical grid for iPhone and Mac. Tap or drag on the grid to place notes, press play, and it loops. The whole grid is locked to a musical scale, so nothing you tap can sound wrong - no lessons, no theory, no wrong notes.

Need help, found a bug, or want a feature? Email leonfigueira@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who wrote the app. You can also use Send feedback in Settings inside the app.

Getting started

  • Tap a square to place a note, or drag across the grid to paint a run of notes.
  • Press play and your pattern loops. Set the tempo in BPM.
  • Tap Sound to switch instruments, and More sounds to add more.
  • Bring in drums, then balance notes and drums with the two sliders.
  • Start from a template for an instant groove, or clear the grid and build your own.

Common questions

How can it be that I can't play a wrong note? Every row of the grid is mapped to a note in a musical scale (a pentatonic scale by default), and there are no notes in between. Whatever you tap is already in key, so anything you make sounds musical - on purpose.

How do I use my own sound? Tap record and make a sound into the microphone - a word, a hum, a tap on the desk. Gridsong turns it into an instrument you can play across the whole grid. Recorded sounds are made and kept entirely on your device.

Do my patterns sync between iPhone and Mac? Yes, if you turn on iCloud sync in Settings. Your patterns sync through your own iCloud account. Sounds you record yourself stay on the device that made them.

How do I share what I made? Use Record a video to capture your loop playing, then share the video anywhere or save it to your photos. Sharing only happens when you choose it.

Does it work offline? Completely. Gridsong needs no account and no connection. There are no ads and no tracking of any kind.

Is there a subscription? No, and there never will be. Gridsong is a single purchase that covers both iPhone and Mac, and every future update is included.
