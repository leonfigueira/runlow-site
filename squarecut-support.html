Squarecut - Support

Squarecut is a feet-inch-fraction calculator for iPhone and iPad. Type a measurement the way you write it, every length stays an exact fraction, and the answer is one you can cut to.

Need help, found a bug, or want a feature? Email leonfigueira@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings, and you will get a reply from the person who wrote it.

Getting started

  • Type the number, then Feet or Inch. 12 Feet 6 Inch gives 12' 6".
  • For a fraction, use the - key between the whole inches and the fraction: 3 - 1 / 2 Inch gives 3-1/2". Without it, 31/2" is fifteen and a half inches, and the app will happily believe you.
  • mm enters millimeters. 2400 mm becomes 7' 10-1/2", marked ≈ because it does not land exactly on a sixteenth.
  • Everything you finish goes on the tape. Tap any row to use that result again.

Common questions

What does the ≈ mean? The exact answer does not fit the fraction you are working in. 1' divided by 7 is not a sixteenth of anything, so the app shows the nearest sixteenth and marks it, instead of quietly rounding. Change the fraction with the pill above the display, or in Settings, and every number on the tape re-renders to match.

Why does 12' times 10' come out in square feet? Because it is an area. A length times a length is an area, three lengths are a volume (shown with cubic yards alongside, for concrete), and the app will refuse to add an area to a length rather than give you a number that means nothing.

How does the percent key work? After plus or minus it takes a share of the running total: 468 sq ft + 10 % = adds a ten percent waste factor and gives 514.8 sq ft. After times or divide it is a plain factor, as on any calculator.

The display shows a number I did not type. You tapped a tape row, or sent a solver result to the calculator. That value is now the entry; press C to take it back without losing the sum you were in the middle of.

What is C and what is AC? The same key. It reads C while there is something to clear, the entry or a refused keystroke, and AC when the next press would clear the whole sum. The tape is never touched by either; Clear tape is separate and asks first.

Is there a subscription? No, and there never will be. The calculator, the tape, every unit and every fraction are free and do not expire. The four solvers, stairs, roof, board feet and layout, unlock together for a single payment that covers every device signed in with your Apple Account.

I paid but the solvers are locked. Settings, then Restore a previous purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought them on. Buying and restoring need a connection once; everything else works with no signal.

What do the solvers assume? Stairs uses the fewest risers that keep each one at or under your maximum, and warns when a riser or tread falls outside the usual residential limits. Roof gives the rafter line length to the center of the ridge: deduct half the ridge and add your tail before you cut. Board feet works on nominal size, so enter 2 x 6 for a 2x6. Layout counts both ends and reports the short last bay.

Does it work with a keyboard on iPad? Digits, plus, minus, times, slash for a fraction, the apostrophe for feet, the quote mark for inches, m for millimeters, Return for equals and Escape to clear. A hyphen straight after a digit is the fraction separator; anywhere else it subtracts.

Accessibility

Squarecut works with VoiceOver: measurements are read as words ("twelve feet six inches"), the answer is announced when you press equals, and a refused key says so. Type follows the Larger Text setting, and the keys stay at least 44 points tall on every iPhone. If something is hard to read or hard to reach, please email. That is a bug worth fixing.
