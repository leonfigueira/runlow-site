GainScout - Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026

GainScout has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. You can use the whole app without telling it who you are. The few times the app does talk to a server, it is described in full below.

What stays on your device

Your saved sites, statuses, notes, visit checklists, evidence photos and reports are stored on your device. While iCloud sync is on (see below) they also sync through your own iCloud. Reports you export as a PDF go wherever you choose to share them. If you share a land check as a link, the check's name, location and findings travel inside the link itself, to whoever you send it to.

Location, camera and photos

If you allow location access, your position is used on your device to jump the map to where you are and to attach a grid reference to field notes and evidence photos. If you allow the camera or photo library, it is only to capture or import evidence photos for a site. None of this is sent to me except as part of an iCloud sync to your own account, or a report you choose to share.

Official data requests

A land check reads official public services live - planning.data.gov.uk (which draws together Natural England and other government data) and the open data services of Natural England and Defra - and uses Apple Maps for the base map, place search and the name of the spot under the pin. Those services receive the same standard information any map app sends: your IP address and the point being checked, or what you type when you search for a place. GainScout adds nothing to identify you, and none of those requests pass through any server of mine. Looking up the biodiversity gain sites register sends nothing, because a dated copy of it is kept inside the app.

Register updates and notices

So the app stays current without waiting for an App Store update, it downloads two public files from GitHub Pages, where these support pages are hosted:

  • A newer copy of the gain sites register, checked for at most once a day. It is only used if it is newer than the copy you already have.
  • Notices from me, such as a known issue or a fix on the way, checked for when the app opens and every few hours. You can turn these off in the Account tab, and then they are not downloaded at all.

Both are plain downloads of the same file for everyone. They send nothing about you, your land or your sites; GitHub only sees what any web request carries, such as your IP address.

iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is on unless you turn it off. While it is on, your sites, statuses, notes, checklists, photos and reports sync between your own iPhone, iPad and Mac through your private iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit. This data lives in your Apple account, managed and secured by Apple - I cannot see it, and it never reaches a server of mine. You can turn sync off at any time in the app's Account tab.

Feedback and support codes

Two features do send a small amount of data to a server I run (hosted on Supabase), and only when you choose to use them:

  • Sending feedback (Account → Report a bug or share an idea) sends your message, the category you picked, your app version, your iOS or macOS version and device model (such as "iPhone16,2"), an optional email address if you add one so I can reply, and a random per-install identifier. It is used only to read and answer your feedback.
  • Redeeming a support code sends the code and that same random per-install identifier, so a single-use code can't be used twice. No message or contact details are sent.

The random identifier is generated on your device, is not linked to your name, Apple ID or email, and cannot be used to identify you personally. This data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for tracking.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and report purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I receive no personal information about you from them, only anonymous sales totals.

Children

GainScout is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, apart from an email address you choose to add to feedback.
